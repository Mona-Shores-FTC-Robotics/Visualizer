Ultraviolet & Infrared Sim Lab

The match simulator that teams Ultraviolet (20245) and Infrared (19429) use to test Autos, drill drivers and check ideas before they reach the robot. Its engine is NCSSM FTC's BIOBUZZ simulator; our robot, our controls and the TIP timing we measured on film sit on top of it.

What we changed

RobotOur robot from the CAD: its frame, FLOWER stick, turret and launcher, with speeds and pick times from the Fruitport and Belding logs.
ButtonsThe drill's controls are read from our robot code's controls export, so every button does what it does on the robot.
TIPTIP lag and spill fitted to 16 films of real CELLs. NCSSM's timing is one switch away, because ours may be wrong.
AutoFull Match replays our seven Autos from the Java simulator's runs of the robot's own code.
